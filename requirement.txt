PROJECT REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

1. INTERVIEW & LOCATION
--------------------------------------------------
- Interview Option: Let users choose "Google Meet" or "On-Site (At Company)" when scheduling.
- Auto Google Meet: Automatically create a Google Meet link when Google Meet is selected.
- Google Maps: Let companies paste a Google Maps URL to show an embedded map.


2. COMPANY & JOBS
--------------------------------------------------
- National ID Card: Require companies to upload a National ID Card before posting jobs.
- AI Job Creator: Add an AI option to automatically write job details when creating a new job.

3. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
- Detail View: Make notifications clickable so users can open and read the full details.

4. DASHBOARD & USERS
--------------------------------------------------
- Dashboard KPI: Show a card for "Total Completed Job Applications".
- Sub-User Accounts: Let companies create employee sub-user accounts under their company.
- Super Admin Access: Give the Super Admin full permissions for everything.
- Anti-Spam Limit: Limit dashboard chart API calls to a maximum of 5 requests to stop spam.
- Fix Users Page: Remove the raw JSON text/message showing on the Users page.


5. LANGUAGE & FONT
--------------------------------------------------
- Khmer & English: Support switching between Khmer (KH) and English (EN) for both menus and saved data.
- Khmer Font: Change the font from Google Sans to a clean Khmer font (like Kantumruy Pro or Noto Sans Khmer).
